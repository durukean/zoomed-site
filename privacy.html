Privacy policy

ZOOMED is a photo-guessing game. It is built to work without knowing anything about you.

The short version

We collect nothing. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no data leaves your device.

Data stored on your device

Your scores, daily streak, stats, collections and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they are deleted when you delete the app.

Duels

Online duels use Apple’s Game Center. When you play one, Game Center shares your Game Center nickname, the 5 photos and your score with your opponent, so the two of you can compare results. Matchmaking, invitations and notifications are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy — we don’t receive or store any of it. Code duels contain only the list of photos and a score, and are shared only when you choose to send them.

Leaderboards, achievements and reminders

If you’re signed in to Game Center, your best scores and achievements are posted to Apple’s Game Center, where your Game Center friends and other players can see them. The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device — nothing is sent to a server.

Children

ZOOMED is suitable for all ages and does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
