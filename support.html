Thanks for playing ZOOMED — how well do you know the world?

How to play

It starts up close

Every photo opens as an extreme close-up.

It slowly pulls back

Points drop as more of the photo is revealed. Answer early for more points — right at the start earns an Eagle-eye bonus.

Stuck? Hold to zoom out

Hold the photo (or the button) to pull back faster. You get one guess per photo.

Modes

Daily Zoom — 10 photos, the same for everyone, once a day. Endless — keep going and beat your best. Survival — the photo never fully zooms out, and one miss ends the run. Mystery — no category, it could be anything. Duel — play 5 photos, send the code to a friend; they get the exact same photos and crops.

Questions

How do I accept a duel? Copy the message your friend sent, open ZOOMED → Duel → Paste.

I left the Daily halfway. It’s saved — tap the Daily card to continue.

My progress disappeared. Progress lives on your device; deleting the app resets it.

Can I turn off sounds or vibration? Yes — Settings on the home screen.
